# GEO Audit Report: repline.io

**Date:** 2026-10-26
**Scope:** GEO / AEO — how well the site can be read, extracted and cited by AI
answer engines (ChatGPT search, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Claude, Gemini)
**Pages Analyzed:** 27 (every sitemap URL) plus `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`, `llms.txt`
**GEO Score:** 74/100 before this run's fixes, **80/100** after them

## Scope and method

Run against a **local production build** (`next build` + `next start`) at `main`
commit `cea299a`. The live host is still unreachable from the audit environment
(the egress proxy refuses `repline.io`), so nothing here measures what AI engines
actually return for any query. This report audits the *inputs* those engines
read, not their outputs.

Tooling: the `seo-geo-optimizer` skill's `audit_report.py`, `analyze_content.py`,
`citation_enhancer.py`, `freshness_monitor.py` and `entity_extractor.py` on every
page, plus a purpose-built pass extracting, per page, the lede after the `<h1>`,
question-form headings, quantified claims, outbound source domains, rendered
`<table>` count, JSON-LD types and dates.

**A caution on the skill's numbers.** Its scripts attach fixed uplift figures
("+41% citation improvement", "3.2x fewer citations after 30 days", "add MD/PhD
credentials for 40%"). Those are generic heuristics from studies of other
corpora, not measurements of this site, and some do not apply at all (there are
no medical credentials to add to a hockey CRM). They are used below only as
pointers to *where* to look; no finding rests on them. This is the same
discipline the 2026-10-22 report asked for: a number is not a finding until it
has been tested.

## Fixed in this run

### 1. Every markdown table on the blog rendered as a paragraph of pipes — High

`src/lib/blog.ts` ran posts through `remark().use(html)`. Plain `remark` is
CommonMark, which has no tables; GFM tables are an extension. So this:

```
| | NHLPA Certified Agent | Hockey Advisor |
|---|---|---|
| Can negotiate NHL contracts | Yes | No |
```

shipped to readers and crawlers as one `<p>` containing literal `|---|---|---|`
text. Affected:

| Post | Tables | GSC impressions (Jul–Oct) |
|---|---|---|
| `/blog/nhlpa-agent-certification-guide` | 1 | **192** — 2nd-largest page on the site |
| `/blog/chl-ncaa-eligibility-rules-2025` | 2 | 65 |
| `/blog/best-crm-for-hockey-agents` | 1 | new |

- **Why it matters for GEO specifically:** comparison tables are among the most
  extracted structures in AI answers — "agent vs. advisor", "CHL vs. NCAA", "best
  CRM for hockey agents" are exactly the questions these tables answer. As pipe
  soup they were unparseable as tables by any engine, and unreadable to people.
- **Fix:** added `remark-gfm` to the pipeline (`remark().use(gfm).use(html)`).
- **Verified:** rebuilt and diffed the rendered `<article>` of all 11 posts
  before/after. Only the three posts above changed, and only in
  `table/thead/tbody/tr/th/td` (replacing the old `<p>`). 4 tables now render, 0
  raw-pipe paragraphs remain. Screenshot-checked: the typography plugin styles
  them correctly.
- **Correction to earlier reports:** this has been true since the April posts
  went live and all six previous audits missed it, including the 2026-10-22
  claim of "readability 14/15 … tables". Those audits counted tables in the
  markdown source, not in the rendered HTML.

### 2. Structured pricing omitted the "Most popular" plan and called Enterprise free — Medium

The `SoftwareApplication` JSON-LD on `/` and `/pricing` listed three offers:
Pro $75, Agency $695, and **Enterprise at `price: "0"`**. The pricing page sells
four plans, with **Team ($249)** badged "Most popular". `llms.txt` lists all four
correctly, so the two machine-readable sources contradicted each other.

- **Why it matters:** an AI engine reading the JSON-LD would report that Repline
  has no plan between $75 and $695, and that Enterprise costs $0. Price questions
  ("how much does Repline cost", "hockey agent CRM pricing") are among the most
  common answer-engine queries for software.
- **Fix:** added the Team offer; removed the Enterprise offer, which has no
  price to state (custom pricing stays on the page, in `llms.txt` and in the FAQ).
  Normalised Agency to `"695.00"`.
- **Verified:** parsed the rendered JSON-LD on both pages: Pro 75.00, Team
  249.00, Agency 695.00.

## Open findings

### 3. Nothing on the homepage says what Repline is — Medium

The homepage `<h1>` is "The CRM built for hockey agents and advisors" and the
lede is "Track players, contracts, scouting reports… Replace your spreadsheets —
from bantam to the bigs." Neither sentence contains the word *Repline*. The
`/about` lede ("Repline exists because…") and `/features` lede ("Repline replaces
your spreadsheets…") don't define it either.

Answer engines build their description of an entity from definitional sentences
of the form "*X is a Y for Z*". With the brand query already contested by Replo
and HockeyStack (2026-10-22, Finding 2), the site never once hands an engine that
sentence about itself, outside `llms.txt` and schema.

- **Fix:** one sentence near the top of `/` and `/about`, e.g. "Repline is a CRM
  for hockey agents and advisors — players, contracts, scouting and compliance in
  one place." Copy decision for the owner; not changed here.
- **Effort:** Low.

### 4. The 16 commercial pages cite no outside source — Medium (carried)

Unchanged since 2026-09-09. `/hockey-crm`, `/sports-agent-crm`,
`/hockey-agency-management-software` and the seven `/vs/` pages make checkable
claims (NCAA/CHL rules, competitor pricing ranges) with zero outbound links. The
blog shows the pattern works here: posts cite 1–9 distinct domains each.
Perplexity in particular weights pages that cite primary sources.

- **Fix:** link the primary source wherever a commercial page states a rule or a
  competitor's price (NCAA, CHL, NHLPA, competitors' own pricing pages).

### 5. Five posts unrevised for six months — Low

`ahl-echl-contract-negotiation-workflow`, `draft-day-prep-checklist-hockey-advisors`,
`how-hockey-agents-manage-their-roster`, `nhlpa-agent-certification-guide` and
`why-hockey-agents-need-a-crm` date from 8–12 April with no `updated` field.
Only `chl-ncaa-eligibility-rules-2025` has been revised. The NHLPA guide is the
priority: 192 impressions, the open "confirm the NHLPA figures" item from
2026-10-22, and now a rendered comparison table.

- **Fix:** genuine review, then set `updated:` in front matter (the template
  already renders it visibly and in `dateModified`). Don't bump dates without
  changing content.

### 6. Six `/vs/` pages have no FAQ schema — Low

`/vs/the-hockey-crm` emits `FAQPage` with 12 question headings; the other six
comparison pages carry two question headings each ("Looking for a HubSpot
alternative?") and no FAQ block. "X alternative" and "X vs Y" are prime
answer-engine query shapes.

- **Fix:** a 3–5 question FAQ per page, sourced from real switching questions,
  wired to the existing `FAQPageSchema` component. Only where the answers are
  substantive — not as filler.

### 7. AI referral traffic is unmeasured — Low

The privacy policy commits to no third-party analytics, and Search Console does
not report ChatGPT, Perplexity or Claude referrals. So there is currently no way
to know whether any of this work produces visits.

- **Fix (privacy-compatible):** count `Referer` hosts (`chatgpt.com`,
  `perplexity.ai`, `claude.ai`, `gemini.google.com`, `copilot.microsoft.com`) and
  `utm_source=chatgpt.com` in the host's request logs. No client-side script needed.

### 8. No IndexNow — Low

Bing's index feeds ChatGPT search and Copilot. IndexNow pushes changed URLs to
Bing on deploy instead of waiting for a crawl. The skill ships
`scripts/indexnow_submit.py` (`--generate-key --output ./public`, then submit
on deploy). Not set up here because submission needs the live host and a key
the owner controls.

### 9. No `sameAs` (carried)

Unchanged; still blocked on social profiles or G2/Capterra listings existing.
When they do, add them to `OrganizationSchema` — it is the cleanest
machine-readable way to separate Repline from Replo.

## What is already strong

- **Access:** nothing blocks AI crawlers, every page is server-rendered HTML,
  `llms.txt` lists every page, plan and guide.
- **Q&A structure:** 16 pages emit `FAQPage` (61 pairs) matching visible content;
  the category pages (`/hockey-crm`, `/sports-agent-crm`,
  `/hockey-agency-management-software`) open with 47–54-word definitional ledes
  that read as quotable answers.
- **Honesty signals:** `aggregateRating` correctly withheld at one review; the
  `/vs/the-hockey-crm` page declines to invent a feature table it can't verify.
  These are exactly the claims an engine can cross-check, and they hold up.
- **Brand title:** the homepage title now leads with the brand
  (`Repline — Hockey Agent & Advisor CRM | Players & Contracts`, 58 chars), per
  2026-10-22 Finding 2.
