# SEO Audit Report: repline.io

**Date:** 2026-09-22
**Pages Analyzed:** 14
**Overall Score:** 74/100 (Needs Improvement — see caveat below)

## Scope and method

This run was performed against a **local production build** (`next build` +
`next start` on `localhost:3000`) at commit `f40ba54`, not against the live
site. Two reasons:

1. The live host `www.repline.io` is unreachable from the audit environment
   (egress proxy denies the CONNECT). Noted as an environment limitation, not
   a site fault.
2. The changes under review — the testimonial system on `/`, `/pricing` and
   `/contact-sales`, and the `Review` JSON-LD on `SoftwareApplication` — are
   on branch `claude/zen-goldberg-1erdr7` and are not yet deployed. Auditing
   production would not have covered them.

Everything below was measured against rendered HTML from the production
build. Where a metric could not be measured, it is marked as such rather
than estimated.

**Caveat on the overall score:** two of the five category inputs are
unmeasured this run (Core Web Vitals and backlink profile). The 74 is
therefore pessimistic — it is depressed by absent data, not by observed
faults. Per-page validation scored every page 90–96 ("Excellent") with
**zero critical issues site-wide**.

## High Priority Issues

1. **`/privacy` and `/terms` are `noindex` but listed in `sitemap.xml`** —
   Both pages serve `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, follow">` while
   `src/app/sitemap.ts` submits them at priority 0.3.
   - **Impact:** Google Search Console reports these as "Submitted URL
     marked 'noindex'" coverage errors. It does not harm rankings directly,
     but it is a standing error state in GSC that masks real issues and
     wastes crawl budget on URLs you have asked not to be indexed.
   - **Fix:** Decide which signal you mean. Either drop the two entries
     from `src/app/sitemap.ts` (keeps them reachable via footer links,
     which is the normal treatment for legal pages), or remove the
     `noindex` if you want them indexed. Removing from the sitemap is the
     usual choice.
   - **Effort:** Low

## Medium Priority Issues

2. **`public/llms.txt` misstates pricing and omits a tier** — The file AI
   crawlers are pointed at contains figures that no longer match the site:

   | Claim in `llms.txt` | Actual (per `src/components/pricing-cards.tsx`) |
   |---|---|
   | Pro — "up to 50 players" | 30 players, 1 user |
   | Agency — "unlimited players" | 200 players, 15 users |
   | *(Team tier absent entirely)* | Team — $249/mo, 75 players, 5 users |
   | 4 comparison URLs listed | 5 exist (`/vs/fandelo` missing) |

   - **Impact:** This is the highest-leverage accuracy problem on the site.
     `robots.txt` explicitly welcomes GPTBot, ClaudeBot and PerplexityBot,
     and `llms.txt` is the file those systems are most likely to treat as
     authoritative. Wrong caps propagate into AI answers about your
     pricing, and a missing mid-tier removes your $249 option from any
     AI-generated comparison.
   - **Fix:** Update the Pricing and Comparisons sections of
     `public/llms.txt` to match `pricing-cards.tsx`. Consider generating
     the pricing block from the same source as the pricing page so it
     cannot drift again.
   - **Effort:** Low

3. **Four page titles exceed the ~60 character SERP limit**
   - `/` — 76 chars: "Repline | Hockey Agent & Advisor CRM — Manage Players, Contracts & Pipelines"
   - `/vs/salesforce` — 75 chars
   - `/vs/hubspot` — 67 chars
   - `/features` — 66 chars
   - **Impact:** Google truncates the tail. On the homepage, "Manage Players,
     Contracts & Pipelines" is cut, so the descriptive half of the title is
     lost in results.
   - **Fix:** Trim to 50–60. E.g. homepage → "Repline | Hockey Agent &
     Advisor CRM" (38) or "Hockey Agent & Advisor CRM | Repline" (36),
     leaving room for the keyword phrase to sit earlier.
   - **Effort:** Low (editorial decision — these are brand-voice choices)

4. **Five meta descriptions exceed ~160 characters** — `/vs/fandelo` (203),
   `/vs/monday` (180), `/blog/why-hockey-agents-need-a-crm` (179), `/` (174),
   `/features` (165).
   - **Impact:** Truncated in SERPs; the CTA at the end of the homepage
     description ("Free 30-day trial") is the part being cut.
   - **Fix:** Trim to 150–160, keeping the CTA inside the limit.
   - **Effort:** Low

5. **`/pricing` skips a heading level** — The outline runs `h1` →
   `h3` (plan names: Pro, Team, Agency, Enterprise) with no intervening
   `h2`. Every other page nests correctly.
   - **Impact:** Minor accessibility and semantic-structure issue; screen
     readers announce a gap in the outline.
   - **Fix:** Add an `h2` above the plan grid in `src/app/pricing/page.tsx`
     (e.g. "Plans"), or demote the card titles. A `SectionHeader` above
     `<PricingCards />` would match the pattern used elsewhere.
   - **Effort:** Low

## Low Priority Issues

6. **`Review` JSON-LD has no `reviewRating`** — Introduced by this branch.
   The `Review` on `SoftwareApplication` carries `reviewBody` and `author`
   but no rating, because no real rating exists to publish.
   - **Impact:** Google's review snippet spec lists `reviewRating` as
     required, so this review is not eligible for a rich result and may
     appear in GSC's Review Snippets report as "missing field". It does not
     affect indexing, and the markup remains valid schema.org — useful for
     entity understanding and AI parsing regardless.
   - **Fix:** Ask Andrew Yarema for a 1–5 rating. `src/lib/testimonials.ts`
     already supports a `rating` field, and `buildReviewFields()` in
     `src/components/schema-org.tsx` will then emit both `reviewRating` and
     an `aggregateRating` automatically. Alternatively drop the `review`
     block until a rating exists. Do not invent a rating to satisfy the
     spec.
   - **Effort:** Low (blocked on the customer, not on code)

7. **`sitemap.xml` reports every static page as modified at build time** —
   `src/app/sitemap.ts` uses `lastModified: new Date()` for all 13 static
   entries, so every deploy marks every page as freshly updated. Blog posts
   correctly use their own front-matter date.
   - **Impact:** Google discounts `lastmod` values it learns not to trust.
     Accurate dates on a handful of pages are worth more than a blanket
     "today" on all of them.
   - **Fix:** Use a real per-page date, or drop `lastModified` from the
     static entries and keep it only where it is meaningful.
   - **Effort:** Low

8. **No social profile links or `sameAs` array** — Unchanged since the
   2026-09-09 report. No Twitter/X, LinkedIn, Facebook or Instagram links
   anywhere on the site; the `Organization` schema has no `sameAs`.
   - **Impact:** Weakens entity consolidation in the Knowledge Graph.
   - **Fix:** Add `sameAs` to `OrganizationSchema` with whatever profiles
     exist, and link them in the footer.
   - **Effort:** Low

9. **AVIF not enabled for image optimization** — Confirmed by content
   negotiation: `Accept: image/webp` returns WebP (58 KB for the hero at
   w=1920), `Accept: image/avif` falls back to PNG (93 KB). Next.js 16
   defaults `images.formats` to `['image/webp']`.
   - **Impact:** Roughly 20% additional compression available on the hero
     screenshot, which is the likely LCP element.
   - **Fix:** Optional. Next's own documentation still recommends WebP for
     most cases, notes AVIF takes ~50% longer to encode on first request,
     and warns that caching both formats increases storage. Enable via
     `images: { formats: ['image/avif', 'image/webp'] }` in `next.config.ts`
     only if you want to trade first-request latency for transfer size.
   - **Effort:** Low

10. **`/contact-sales` has no breadcrumbs** — It is the only non-homepage
    route without a `Breadcrumbs` component and therefore without
    `BreadcrumbList` schema.
    - **Fix:** Add `<Breadcrumbs items={[{label:"Home",href:"/"},{label:"Talk to Us"}]} />`
      to match the other pages.
    - **Effort:** Low

## Detailed Findings

### Crawlability & Indexation — 74/100

- **robots.txt:** Valid. Allows all agents, with explicit `Allow` for
  GPTBot, ClaudeBot and PerplexityBot. Sitemap declared.
- **Sitemap:** 23 URLs, valid XML. Coverage is complete — 13 static routes
  plus all 10 blog posts in `content/blog/`. No 404s among them; all 14
  sampled URLs returned 200.
- **Canonicals:** Self-referencing and correct on all 14 pages checked. No
  conflicts.
- **Indexation:** Only `/privacy` and `/terms` carry `noindex, follow`, and
  both are in the sitemap (finding 1). All 12 commercially relevant pages
  are `index, follow`.
- **URL structure:** Clean, lowercase, hyphenated, logical `/vs/*` and
  `/blog/*` hierarchies. No parameters.
- **Crawl depth:** Every page is within two clicks of the homepage via nav
  and footer.
- **404:** Custom `not-found.tsx` present.

### Technical Foundations — 88/100 (Core Web Vitals unmeasured)

- **Core Web Vitals / PageSpeed:** **Unable to measure.** The PageSpeed
  Insights API returned HTTP 429 (daily quota exhausted for the anonymous
  project). Manual check recommended via
  `https://pagespeed.web.dev/?url=https://www.repline.io`, or supply an API
  key for a future run.
- **Rendering:** All content is present in the server-rendered HTML — every
  page is statically prerendered (`○ (Static)`) except `/og`, which is
  correctly dynamic. Nothing depends on client-side JS to be crawlable.
- **Images:** Handled by `next/image` throughout. WebP negotiation
  verified working. The hero screenshot carries `priority` (correct for
  LCP); the new testimonial logo is correctly lazy-loaded below the fold.
  Source screenshots total 1.7 MB but are never served raw — the optimizer
  reduces the 239 KB hero to 58 KB WebP at w=1920.
- **Security headers:** `next.config.ts` sets `X-Content-Type-Options`,
  `X-Frame-Options`, `Referrer-Policy` and `Permissions-Policy` on all
  routes.
- **Mobile:** Verified responsive at 390 px width — no horizontal scroll,
  layout reflows correctly including the new testimonial card.
- **Caching:** Optimized images return `Cache-Control: public,
  max-age=14400, must-revalidate`.

## Verdict on the changes under review

The testimonial system and its structured data introduced **no SEO
regressions**:

- Heading hierarchy on `/` remains correct — the new `h2` ("Advisors who
  made the switch") sits alongside the existing section headings with
  properly nested content.
- The agency logo has descriptive alt text and is lazily loaded below the
  fold, so it does not compete with the LCP hero image.
- `Review` JSON-LD parses cleanly and is attached to the correct entity
  (`SoftwareApplication`), with `author` modelled as a `Person` with
  `jobTitle` and `worksFor`.
- No `aggregateRating` is emitted, which is correct — the code only emits
  one when every published testimonial carries a real rating, so the site
  cannot claim a score it has not earned.
- The quote appears on three pages. This is not a duplicate-content
  concern at this length relative to each page's unique body copy.
- All three affected pages score 90–96 with zero critical issues.

The one open item is finding 6 (`reviewRating`), which is blocked on the
customer rather than on code.
