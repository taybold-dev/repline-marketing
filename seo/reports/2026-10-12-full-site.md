## High Priority Issues

### 1. Every responsive image ships its largest variant to every device

**19 of 21 `<Image>` components set `width={2880}` with
`className="w-full h-auto"` and no `sizes` prop.** Next.js documents the
consequence precisely, in `node_modules/next/dist/docs/01-app/03-api-reference/02-components/image.md:224`:

> If `sizes` is missing, the browser assumes the image will be as wide as the
> viewport (`100vw`). This can cause unnecessarily large images to be
> downloaded.
>
> - Without `sizes`: Next.js generates a limited `srcset` (e.g. 1x, 2x),
>   suitable for fixed-size images.
> - With `sizes`: Next.js generates a full `srcset` (e.g. 640w, 750w, etc.),
>   optimized for responsive layouts.

The emitted markup confirms it. The homepage hero renders a single-entry
srcSet:

```
srcSet="/_next/image?url=%2Fscreenshots%2Fdashboard.png&w=3840&q=75 1x"
```

There is no 750w or 1080w candidate for a phone to choose. Measured variant
weights, WebP at q=75:

| Screenshot | w=3840 (shipped) | w=750 (needed on a phone) | Ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| `dashboard` **(LCP element)** | 102,376 B | 14,192 B | **7.2×** |
| `tasks-board` | 122,760 B | 17,556 B | 7.0× |
| `contacts-list` | 91,790 B | 12,310 B | 7.5× |
| `settings-team` | 75,238 B | 10,028 B | 7.5× |
| `players-list` | 68,046 B | 10,878 B | 6.3× |
| `player-detail` | 65,918 B | 9,760 B | 6.8× |
| `player-contracts` | 63,378 B | 8,704 B | 7.3× |
| `calendar` | 61,092 B | 8,684 B | 7.0× |
| `comps` | 35,904 B | 4,900 B | 7.3× |

Per-page totals on a 390px viewport:

| Page | Images | Shipped | Needed | Wasted |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `/` | 3 | 236 KB | 35 KB | **201 KB** |
| `/features` | 5 | 412 KB | 59 KB | **353 KB** |
| `/sports-agent-crm` | 1 | 68 KB | 11 KB | 57 KB |
| each `/vs/*` page | 1 | 61–123 KB | 9–18 KB | 52–105 KB |

- **Impact:** This is the first hard evidence of a Core Web Vitals problem on
  this site rather than an absence of data. The LCP element on the homepage is
  a 102 KB image that is also `<link rel="preload">`ed at highest priority —
  so the single largest blocker of LCP on mobile is being fetched as early and
  as aggressively as possible at 7.2× its necessary weight. On a slow 4G
  connection (~1.6 Mbps effective) 102 KB is roughly 510 ms of transfer
  against 71 ms for the right variant. `/features` is worse in aggregate.
- **Fix:** Add `sizes` to each fluid `<Image>`. These screenshots render full
  width inside containers capped at `max-w-4xl` (896px) or `max-w-6xl`
  (1152px), so:
  ```tsx
  sizes="(max-width: 768px) 100vw, (max-width: 1152px) 90vw, 1152px"
  ```
  Files: `src/app/page.tsx` (3), `src/app/features/page.tsx` (5),
  `src/app/about/page.tsx` (1), `src/app/hockey-crm/page.tsx` (1),
  `src/app/sports-agent-crm/page.tsx` (1),
  `src/app/hockey-agency-management-software/page.tsx` (1), and one each in
  the seven `src/app/vs/*/page.tsx`. The two `testimonials.tsx` images are
  genuinely fixed-size (80px avatar, `h-20` logo) and correctly need no
  `sizes`.
- **Effort:** Low — 19 one-line additions, no logic change.
- **Why it was missed three times:** prior runs confirmed WebP negotiation was
  working and stopped there. Format was right; sizing was never checked.

### 2. Four blog titles are truncated in the SERP

| Chars | Page | Title |
|---|---|---|
| 81 | `/blog/nhlpa-agent-certification-guide` | "How to Become an NHLPA Certified Agent: Requirements, Process, and What to Expect" |
| 78 | `/blog/how-many-clients-does-a-hockey-agent-have` | "How Many Clients Does a Hockey Agent Have? A Realistic Look at Roster Capacity" |
| 75 | `/blog/how-hockey-agents-and-advisors-network-with-scouts-coaches` | "How Hockey Agents and Advisors Network With Scouts, Coaches, and Team Staff" |
| 66 | `/blog/ahl-echl-contract-negotiation-workflow` | "AHL and ECHL Contract Negotiation: What Hockey Agents Need to Know" |

All 16 static pages are within 60. This is a blog-only problem, which is why
three audits reported "0 of 16 over length" and were technically right and
practically misleading.

- **Impact:** Google truncates around 60 characters. The NHLPA post loses
  "Requirements, Process, and What to Expect" — 21 characters past the cut —
  and that post is the site's best-linked informational asset (5 contextual
  inbound). The capacity post loses its entire differentiator, "A Realistic
  Look at Roster Capacity". CTR cost only; no ranking cost.
- **Fix:** Trim each to ≤60 while keeping the head term first. Suggested:
  - "How to Become an NHLPA Certified Agent (2026 Guide)" — 50
  - "How Many Clients Does a Hockey Agent Have?" — 42
  - "How Hockey Agents Network With Scouts and Coaches" — 48
  - "AHL and ECHL Contract Negotiation for Hockey Agents" — 50
- **Effort:** Low — four frontmatter edits.

## Medium Priority Issues

### 3. Ten of eleven blog posts have no FAQ schema, and the plumbing is already built

PR #18 added optional `faqs` frontmatter wired to `FAQPageSchema` in
`src/app/blog/[slug]/page.tsx`. Exactly one post uses it:
`best-crm-for-hockey-agents` (5 entries). The other ten pass `faqs=0`.

- **Impact:** This is the cheapest AEO/GEO work on the board. `FAQPage` is
  what gets lifted into AI Overviews and assistant answers, and the
  informational posts are precisely the pages those engines cite. Several
  posts are already question-shaped — `how-many-clients-does-a-hockey-agent-have`
  is a question in its slug and has no FAQ block.
- **Fix:** Add 3–5 `faqs` entries per post, drawn from each post's existing H2s
  and from People Also Ask. Zero code change; frontmatter only.
- **Effort:** Low per post, medium in aggregate (ten files).

### 6. Core Web Vitals: the blind spot is now structural, not transient

Three audits recorded "HTTP 429 — daily quota exhausted" and implied a retry
would work. It will not. The API response this run names the real cause:

```
"quota_limit": "defaultPerDayPerProject",
"quota_limit_value": "0"
```

The per-day quota for unauthenticated PageSpeed Insights calls from this
environment is **zero**, not exhausted. No amount of waiting changes it.

- **Impact:** Four audits with no field data on the one category where
  synthetic analysis is weakest. Finding 1 shows this is not hypothetical.
- **Fix:** Either (a) connect Search Console, which supplies real-user CWV
  from the Chrome UX Report — the single highest-value unblock available and
  now requested six times, or (b) provide a PageSpeed Insights API key as
  `PAGESPEED_API_KEY`, or (c) run `pagespeed.web.dev/analyze?url=https://www.repline.io`
  in a browser and paste the result.
- **Effort:** Low, but it is not mine to do.

### 7. `llms.txt` lists no blog posts

`public/llms.txt` is current and accurate on pricing (verified against
`/pricing`: $75/$249/$695, annual $64/$212/$583) and lists all 16 static
pages. It lists **zero of the 11 blog posts** — only `/blog` itself.

- **Impact:** The file exists to tell AI crawlers what matters, and it omits
  every page that answers a question. The blog is where GPTBot, ClaudeBot and
  PerplexityBot — all explicitly allowed in `robots.txt` — would find citable
  material.
- **Fix:** Add a `## Guides` section listing each post with a one-line
  description. Better, generate it from `src/lib/blog.ts` at build time so it
  cannot drift, the way `sitemap.ts` already does.
- **Effort:** Low manually, medium to automate. Automate it — three audits of
  drift on this file argue against hand-maintenance.

### 8. Static sitemap entries now carry no date signal at all

Last audit's Finding 3 said "use a real per-page date, **or** drop
`lastModified`". We dropped it, which fixed the always-today problem and left
16 pages with no freshness signal. Blog entries correctly use
`new Date(post.updated ?? post.date)`.

- **Impact:** Minor and strictly better than before. But `/vs/monday`,
  `/vs/hubspot` and `/vs/salesforce` all had pricing corrected on
  2026-10-06 and the sitemap does not say so.
- **Fix:** Derive `lastModified` per route from git (`git log -1 --format=%cI
  -- <path>`) at build time.
- **Effort:** Medium.

## Low Priority Issues

### 9. Six blog descriptions exceed 160 characters

223, 184, 178, 174, 174 and 162 characters. The 223 on

second clause. Descriptions are not a ranking factor and Google rewrites most

### 10. `priority` is deprecated in Next.js 16

`src/app/page.tsx:74` passes `priority`. Per
`node_modules/next/dist/docs/01-app/03-api-reference/02-components/image.md:291`:

> Starting with Next.js 16, the `priority` property has been deprecated in
> favor of the `preload` property in order to make the behavior clear.

The same docs then advise against `preload` for this case: *"In most cases,
you should use `loading="eager"` or `fetchPriority="high"` instead of
`preload`."* Works today, no build warning, but it is on a deprecation path.

- **Fix:** Replace `priority` with `loading="eager" fetchPriority="high"`, and
  add the `sizes` prop from Finding 1 at the same time.

### 11. No `sameAs` array, no social profile links

Unchanged since 2026-09-09. `Organization` schema has no `sameAs`; grep finds
no link to any social profile anywhere in `src/`. Scored 1/10 on social
signals.

### 12. Zero outbound citations on all 16 commercial pages

The blog cites 2–10 external sources per post. The static pages cite none —
including pages making verifiable factual claims about NCAA rules, CHL
eligibility and competitor pricing. The competitor pricing claims corrected
on 2026-10-06 are written as approximations with verify notes in the source
comments, but a reader sees an unsourced number.

- **Fix:** Cite the NCAA and CHL rule sources on `/sports-agent-crm`,
  `/hockey-crm` and `/hockey-agency-management-software`; link each
  competitor's own pricing page from its `/vs/` page.

### 13. The 404 page inherits the site's default title

`/does-not-exist-xyz` returns a correct 404 with a "Page not found" `<h1>`,
but `<title>` is the layout's 78-character default rather than something like
"Page not found | Repline". 404s are not indexed; cosmetic only.

### 14. AVIF not enabled

Unchanged and still a deliberate tradeoff. Note that fixing Finding 1 delivers
far more than AVIF would: correct `sizes` cuts the homepage hero 7.2×, where
AVIF over WebP would cut perhaps 20%.
