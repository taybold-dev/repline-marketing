## High Priority Issues

### 1. Blog cross-linking runs one way

The four posts added in commit `b49c143` link out generously to the older
posts — 3 to 6 contextual links each — but **no older post links back to any
of them**. Their only internal inbound link is the blog index:

| Post | Inbound | Outbound to other posts |
|------|---------|-------------------------|
| `how-hockey-agents-advisors-find-sign-new-clients` | 1 | 6 |
| `how-hockey-agents-and-advisors-network-with-scouts-coaches` | 1 | 3 |
| `how-many-clients-does-a-hockey-agent-have` | 1 | 4 |
| `why-hockey-players-leave-their-agent` | 1 | 6 |
| *(for contrast)* `nhlpa-agent-certification-guide` | 8 | 2 |
| *(for contrast)* `chl-ncaa-eligibility-rules-2025` | 7 | 0 |
| *(for contrast)* `how-hockey-agents-manage-their-roster` | 7 | 1 |

The newer posts were written with the older cluster in mind and reference it
throughout. The older posts were never updated to point back, so link equity
flows in one direction only — out of the new content and into the old.

- **Impact:** These are not orphans, and they are not isolated. They are
  **net donors**: they pass authority to the older posts and receive almost
  none in return, which caps how well four of ten posts can rank no matter
  how good they are. It is also the cheapest ranking work available, since
  the content and most of the links already exist.
- **Fix:** Add reciprocal links from the older cluster. The natural pairings
  are already implied by the outbound links the newer posts make:
  - `how-hockey-agents-manage-their-roster` → `how-many-clients-does-a-hockey-agent-have`
  - `why-hockey-agents-need-a-crm` → `why-hockey-players-leave-their-agent`
  - `nhlpa-agent-certification-guide` → `how-hockey-agents-advisors-find-sign-new-clients`
  - `how-hockey-agents-advisors-find-sign-new-clients` →
    `how-hockey-agents-and-advisors-network-with-scouts-coaches`
    (the one pairing where both ends are newer posts)
- **Effort:** Low. A sentence in each of four Markdown files.
- **Note:** This condition existed at the 2026-09-22 audit and was missed
  there, because that run did not map the link graph.

### 2. `/sports-agent-crm` is under-linked for a Tier 1 commercial page

The new category page has **3 inbound internal links**, against 30+ for every
`/vs/` page and 59 for `/features`.

- **Impact:** It targets two of the four Tier 1 keywords in
  `seo/keyword-targets.md` and currently sits lower in the internal
  hierarchy than any comparison page. Three links is what the plan specified,
  and the plan under-specified.
- **Fix:** Add it to the footer `productLinks` in
  `src/components/footer.tsx` — that alone adds a sitewide link and brings it
  in line with how `/features` and `/pricing` are treated. Consider a link
  from the homepage as well.
- **Effort:** Low.

## Medium Priority Issues

### 3. Sitemap still reports every static page as modified at build time

`src/app/sitemap.ts` uses `lastModified: new Date()` for all 13 static
entries. Carried forward unchanged from 2026-09-22.

- **Impact:** Google learns to distrust `lastmod` values that always say
  "now". Accurate dates on a few pages are worth more than a blanket today
  on all of them.
- **Fix:** Use a real per-page date, or drop `lastModified` from static
  entries and keep it only where meaningful (blog posts already use their
  front-matter date correctly).
- **Effort:** Low.

### 4. CHL-NCAA post still carries no NIL coverage

`content/blog/chl-ncaa-eligibility-rules-2025.md` is still titled "(2025-26)"
and still mentions NIL **zero times**, three weeks after this was logged as
items #11 and #12 in `seo/keyword-targets.md`.

- **Impact:** It is the site's strongest authority asset on its most
  competitive informational topic, and it is visibly dated in a story that
  moved substantially through 2026.
- **Fix:** Refresh rather than rewrite. The research is already in
  `seo/keyword-targets.md`.
- **Effort:** Medium.

## Detailed Findings

### Crawlability & Indexation — 92/100 (was 74)

The jump is the sitemap/noindex conflict being resolved.

- **robots.txt:** Valid. Allows all agents, with explicit `Allow` for GPTBot,
  ClaudeBot and PerplexityBot. Sitemap declared.
- **Sitemap:** 23 URLs, valid XML, complete coverage of 13 static routes plus
  all 10 blog posts. Zero references to `/privacy` or `/terms`, which are
  `noindex` — the September conflict is resolved and has not regressed.
- **Canonicals:** Self-referencing and correct on **16/16** pages.
- **Indexation:** Only `/privacy` and `/terms` carry `noindex, follow`, and
  neither is submitted. All 14 commercially relevant pages are
  `index, follow`.
- **URL structure:** Clean, lowercase, hyphenated. `/sports-agent-crm` sits
  at root rather than under `/vs/`, which is correct — it is a category page,
  not a comparison.
- **Crawl depth:** Every page within two clicks of the homepage.
- **Deduction:** sitemap `lastmod` (Finding 3).

### Technical Foundations — 88/100 (Core Web Vitals unmeasured)

#### 1. Core Web Vitals have now gone three audits unmeasured

The PageSpeed Insights API returned **HTTP 429 — daily quota exhausted** on
2026-09-22 and again on 2026-10-06, and the 2026-09-09 baseline had no tool
available either. This is no longer an incidental gap; it is a standing blind
spot over the site's entire measured history, covering three of the thirteen
checks in this category.

**Resolving it needs one of:** a PageSpeed API key in the environment, Google
Search Console connected (which also unblocks the Authority category), or a
manual run at `https://pagespeed.web.dev/?url=https://www.repline.io`.

Until then LCP, INP and CLS are unknown, and any Technical score here is
partial by construction.

- **Rendering:** All content present in server-rendered HTML. Every route
  statically prerendered except `/og`, correctly dynamic. Nothing depends on
  client-side JS to be crawlable.
- **Images:** 23 images, all through `next/image`. WebP negotiation verified.
  Hero carries `priority`; below-fold images lazy-load correctly, including
  the Yarema logo and the new pages' screenshots.
- **Security headers:** `X-Content-Type-Options`, `X-Frame-Options`,
  `Referrer-Policy`, `Permissions-Policy` on all routes.
- **Mobile:** Verified responsive at 390px.
- **Deduction:** AVIF (Finding 7).

## Corrections to previous reports

**2026-09-22 scored Internal linking 20/25 without measuring it.** That run
assessed structure qualitatively ("clear hub-and-spoke, no orphan pages")
and did not check inbound link counts per URL. Measuring it this time found
four blog posts with a single inbound link each and no outbound links at all.
The condition predates both reports. The Authority score moving 54 → 50
reflects better measurement, not a decline in the site.

## Correction made during this audit

The first draft of Finding 1 reported that the four posts had **zero
outbound links** as well as one inbound, and described them as cut off from
the graph in both directions. That was wrong. It came from grepping the
Markdown for `repline.io/blog/` when the posts use relative `](/blog/...)`
links, so every outbound link was missed. Re-measured against the rendered
HTML and against `git HEAD`, the four posts carry 3 to 6 outbound links
each.

The inbound half of the finding is unaffected and was measured correctly
from the rendered link graph. The diagnosis changes from "isolated" to
"one-way", and the fix changes from editing both directions to adding the
reciprocal links only.
