## Critical Issues (Fix Immediately)

None found. No noindex on important pages, no broken canonicals, no missing

## High Priority Issues

1. **8 of 19 pages have meta descriptions over 160 characters** - Google
   typically truncates descriptions around 155-160 characters, so the
   truncated version (not what was written) becomes the SERP snippet.
   - **Affected pages and lengths:** `/` (174), `/vs/fandelo` (203),
     `/blog/nhlpa-agent-certification-guide` (184),
     `/blog/chl-ncaa-eligibility-rules-2025` (186),
     `/blog/why-hockey-agents-need-a-crm` (179),
     `/blog/ahl-echl-contract-negotiation-workflow` (174), `/features` (165),
     `/vs/monday` (180)
   - **Worst offender:** `/vs/fandelo` at 203 characters:
     > "Fandelo is a fan-engagement platform with a talent-management app
     > bolted on — not built for hockey representation. Compare it to
     > Repline's e-signatures, PDF scouting profiles, and hockey-native
     > workflow."
   - **Impact:** Snippets get cut mid-sentence, which reads as unfinished
     and can suppress click-through rate on otherwise well-ranking pages.
   - **Fix:** Trim each to 150-160 characters, front-loading the value
     proposition so truncation (if it still happens on mobile) doesn't cut
     the key claim.
   - **Effort:** Low (copy edit only, no code change — descriptions are
     already externalized as page metadata).

## Medium Priority Issues

1. **6 of 19 pages have title tags over 60 characters** - Google generally
   displays ~50-60 characters (~600px) before truncating.
   - **Affected pages and lengths:** `/` (76),
     `/blog/nhlpa-agent-certification-guide` (81), `/vs/salesforce` (75),
     `/vs/hubspot` (67), `/features` (66),
     `/blog/ahl-echl-contract-negotiation-workflow` (66)
   - **Worst offender:** the NHLPA blog post title, 81 characters:
     "How to Become an NHLPA Certified Agent: Requirements, Process, and
     What to Expect"
   - **Impact:** Truncated titles in search results and browser tabs; the
     part that gets cut is often the differentiator (e.g. the homepage
     title cuts off before "Pipelines").
   - **Fix:** Tighten to 50-60 characters. E.g. homepage could drop to
     "Repline | Hockey Agent & Advisor CRM" (37 chars) and let the meta
     description carry the rest of the value prop.
   - **Effort:** Low.

2. **`sitemap.xml` lists 2 URLs that are `noindex`** - `/privacy` and
   `/terms` both correctly carry `<meta name="robots" content="noindex,
   follow">`, but they're still listed in the sitemap.
   - **Impact:** Minor — sends mixed signals to crawlers and wastes a
     sliver of crawl budget on pages that will never be indexed. Not
     harmful, just inconsistent.
   - **Fix:** Exclude `/privacy` and `/terms` from sitemap generation, or
     accept the inconsistency (common in Next.js sites where sitemap and
     the noindex flag are generated separately) — either is defensible.
   - **Effort:** Low.

## Low Priority Issues

1. **Custom 404 page doesn't override the `<title>` tag** - `/some-bad-url`
   correctly returns HTTP 404 with a proper "404 / Page not found" H1 and
   body copy, but the `<title>` tag falls back to the homepage's title
   ("Repline | Hockey Agent & Advisor CRM — Manage Players, Contracts &
   Pipelines").
   - **Impact:** Cosmetic — status code and on-page messaging are already
     correct, so this doesn't hurt indexation. Just confusing in a browser
     tab or if the URL gets shared.
   - **Fix:** Set a dedicated `<title>Page Not Found | Repline</title>` in
     the `not-found.tsx` metadata.
   - **Effort:** Low.

2. **`http://repline.io` takes 2 redirect hops to reach the canonical URL**
   (`http://repline.io` → `https://repline.io` → `https://www.repline.io`).
   This is within the "no chains longer than 2 hops" tolerance, so not
   scored as an issue, but it's an easy one-hop savings.
   - **Fix:** Point the HTTP→HTTPS redirect directly at
     `https://www.repline.io` instead of the apex.
   - **Effort:** Low (hosting/DNS config, not app code).

3. **One blog post has zero images** -
   `/blog/chl-ncaa-eligibility-rules-2025` is text-only; the other five
   posts have 1-3 images each.
   - **Fix:** Add at least one supporting image/diagram for consistency
     and engagement.
   - **Effort:** Low-Medium.

## Quick Wins (High Impact, Low Effort)

1. Trim the 8 over-length meta descriptions to ≤160 characters — pure copy
   edit, no code changes, directly reduces SERP snippet truncation.
2. Shorten the 6 over-length title tags to ≤60 characters, starting with
   the 81-character NHLPA post title.
3. Add a dedicated `<title>` for the 404 page.

## Detailed Findings

### Crawlability & Indexation — 94/100

- **robots.txt** (`https://repline.io/robots.txt`): present, allows all
  crawlers, and explicitly `Allow`s `GPTBot`, `ClaudeBot`, and
  `PerplexityBot` — a deliberate, good choice for AI-search visibility.
  Points to `Sitemap: https://www.repline.io/sitemap.xml`. No issue.
- **XML Sitemap**: valid, 19 URLs, each with `lastmod`/`changefreq`/
  `priority`. Includes 2 noindexed URLs (see Medium Issues above, -3).
- **Canonical tags**: checked on all 19 pages — every single one has a
  self-referencing `<link rel="canonical">` pointing at its own
  `www.repline.io` URL. No conflicts found.
- **Noindex tags**: only present on `/privacy` and `/terms`, both
  intentional. No important page is accidentally noindexed.
- **URL structure**: clean, lowercase, hyphenated slugs throughout
  (`/vs/hubspot`, `/blog/why-hockey-agents-need-a-crm`, etc).
- **Redirect chains**: `http://repline.io` → `https://repline.io` →
  `https://www.repline.io` is a 2-hop chain (within tolerance, see Low
  Issues above).
- **404 handling**: `/this-page-does-not-exist-xyz123` correctly returns
  HTTP 404 with a real "Page not found" H1 and body copy (not a soft 404).
  Only the `<title>` tag is unhandled (see Low Issues above, -3).
- **Crawl depth**: every page in the sitemap is reachable within 1-2 clicks
  from the homepage via the header nav and in-content links.

### Technical Foundations — 92/100

- **HTTPS**: enforced sitewide. `Strict-Transport-Security: max-age=63072000`
  present. Apex domain 308-redirects to `https://www.repline.io`.
- **Security headers**: `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff`,
  `X-Frame-Options: DENY`, `Referrer-Policy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin`,
  `Permissions-Policy: camera=(), microphone=(), geolocation=()` all present.
- **Mobile viewport**: `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width,
  initial-scale=1">` present; markup uses responsive (Tailwind `md:`/`lg:`)
  classing throughout.
- **TTFB**: 113ms on homepage (measured via `curl -w time_starttransfer`) —
  well within the "good" (<200ms) threshold. Response served from Vercel
  edge cache (`X-Vercel-Cache: HIT`, `Age: 148927`).
- **Server-rendered content**: homepage is served pre-rendered
  (`X-Nextjs-Prerender: 1`) with full text content present in the initial
  HTML response — confirmed by fetching with `curl` (no JS execution) and
  finding real page copy, not a JS-only shell. Good for crawlers that don't
  execute JavaScript.
- **Image delivery**: images are served through Next.js's `/_next/image`
  optimizer with explicit `width`/`quality` params, which negotiates
  modern formats (WebP/AVIF) by `Accept` header automatically.
- **Not measured this run:** Core Web Vitals (LCP/INP/CLS) and Google
  PageSpeed Insights scores. The PSI API (`googleapis.com/pagespeedonline`)
  requires an API key that wasn't available in this environment. **This is
  a real gap, not a passing grade** — treat the 92/100 here as provisional
  until a PageSpeed run is done. Manual check: https://pagespeed.web.dev/?url=https://www.repline.io
- **Not verified:** exact compression algorithm (gzip vs. Brotli) and
  HTTP/2 vs HTTP/3 — the `curl` available in this environment doesn't
  support HTTP/2 and didn't return a `Content-Encoding` header on a HEAD
  request, but Vercel serves HTTP/2+Brotli to browsers by default, so this
  is very likely a measurement artifact rather than a real gap. Flagging
  rather than fabricating a confirmed "pass."

### On-Page Optimization — 77/100

- **Title tags**: present and unique on all 19 pages; every page has
  exactly one clear topic. 13/19 are within the 50-60 char guideline; 6
  are over (see Medium Issues).
- **Meta descriptions**: present and unique on all 19 pages. 11/19 are
  within 150-160 chars; 8 are over (see High Issues).
- **H1 tags**: exactly one `<h1>` per page on all 19 pages checked, no
  duplicates or missing H1s. No issue.
- **Image alt text**: 100% coverage — every `<img>` sitewide (checked
  across all 19 pages) has a descriptive, specific `alt` attribute (e.g.
  "Hockey agent player pipeline board with columns for U.S. Junior A, CHL
  Major Junior, NCAA, and ECHL"). This is a genuine strength, not just a
  pass — the alt text is written for humans, not keyword-stuffed.
- **Open Graph / Twitter Card tags**: `og:title`, `og:description`,
  `og:url`, `og:type`, `og:image` (1200x630 with alt text), and
  `twitter:card=summary_large_image` + title/description/image all present
  on every page checked. No issue.
- **Content length**: the sampled blog post
  (`/blog/why-hockey-agents-need-a-crm`) runs to roughly 3,600 words with 8
  H2 subsections — substantial for the topic. Not independently verified
  against competitor word counts (no competitor data available this run).
- **Internal/external links**: homepage links to all primary sections
  (Features, Pricing, Blog, About, Contact, all `/vs/*` pages) via nav and
  footer. The sampled blog post links out to 2 authoritative external
  sources (ncaa.org, nhlpa.com). No orphaned-page evidence found in the 19
  sitemap URLs (all reachable from nav).
- **URL slugs**: clean and descriptive throughout, e.g.
  `/blog/nhlpa-agent-certification-guide`,
  `/vs/spreadsheets`. No issue.

### Content Quality — 66/100

*(Qualitative category; scored on the skill's 0-100 sum of six factors,
not the deduction formula.)*

- **E-E-A-T signals (8/20):** Blog posts attribute authorship to "Repline
  Team" (an `Organization`, per the `BlogPosting` schema's `author` field)
  rather than a named individual with stated credentials. The `/about`
  page describes the product philosophy but doesn't name founders, team
  members, or relevant hockey/agent-industry credentials. Given the
  content touches NCAA/NHLPA eligibility and certification rules — areas
  where readers care about source authority — this is the single biggest
  content-quality gap found. Contact info (`hello@repline.io`) is
  consistent sitewide, which helps somewhat.
- **Content freshness (8/15):** Sitemap `lastmod` dates on blog posts
  cluster around 2026-04-11/12 (roughly 5 months old as of this audit); no
  visible "last updated" indicator in the rendered post content itself, so
  a reader can't tell freshness without checking the sitemap.
- **Content depth (17/20):** The sampled post is genuinely long-form
  (~3,600 words, 8 subsections) with specific, non-generic detail (league
  names, roster levels, workflow specifics) rather than thin filler.
- **Originality (12/15):** Content reads as written specifically for the
  hockey-agent niche rather than reskinned generic SaaS copy — assessed
  qualitatively from the sampled post and homepage; no plagiarism/originality
  tool was run.
- **Readability (11/15):** Short paragraphs and frequent H2 subheadings in
  the sampled post; no Flesch reading-ease score computed (no tool
  available this run).
- **Media richness (10/15):** 5 of 6 blog posts include 1-3 images; one
  (`/blog/chl-ncaa-eligibility-rules-2025`) has none. No video or
  interactive elements found anywhere on the site.

### Authority & Links — 52/100

*(Qualitative category; scored on the skill's 0-100 sum of five factors.)*

- **Internal linking structure (20/25):** Clear hub-and-spoke structure —
  homepage and nav fan out to Features/Pricing/Blog/About/Contact and all
  5 comparison pages; blog index links to all posts. No orphan pages found
  among the 19 sitemap URLs.
- **Anchor text diversity (18/25):** Nav anchor text is appropriately
  generic ("Features", "Pricing"); in-content anchor text on the sampled
  blog post is descriptive rather than "click here"-style. Full sitewide
  anchor-text audit wasn't performed.
- **Backlink profile indicators (0/25, unmeasured):** No backlink data
  source (Ahrefs, Moz, Google Search Console) was available in this
  environment. **This is not a score of zero-quality backlinks — it's zero
  data.** Run the `search-console` skill once GSC access is connected, or
  a dedicated backlink tool, before trusting the Authority & Links total.
- **Social signals (2/10):** No linked social profiles found anywhere on
  the site (no Twitter/X, LinkedIn, Facebook, or Instagram links; the
  `Organization` JSON-LD schema has no `sameAs` array). No share buttons
  on blog posts.
- **Brand mentions / NAP consistency (12/15):** Brand name "Repline" and
  contact email `hello@repline.io` are consistent across every page
  checked. No physical address (not applicable — SaaS, not a local
  business).
